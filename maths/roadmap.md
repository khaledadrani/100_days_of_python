| **Standard / Core Math**                | Comfortable with high school algebra; ready for undergrad intro courses in calc/linear algebra. | 1. **Full calculus I/II**: Derivatives (chain/product/quotient rules), integrals (definite/indefinite, substitution, parts), fundamental theorem, applications (optimization, related rates). <br> 2. **Linear algebra fundamentals**: Systems of equations, Gaussian elimination, vector spaces, eigenvalues/eigenvectors basics. <br> 3. **Probability distributions**: Discrete (binomial, Poisson), continuous (normal, uniform), expectation/variance. <br> 4. **Multivariable functions**: Partial derivatives, gradients, double/triple integrals. <br> 5. **Series and sequences**: Taylor series, convergence tests, Fourier series intro. <br> 6. **Trigonometry and geometry**: Trig identities, polar/parametric equations, conic sections. <br> 7. **Differential equations basics**: First-order ODEs (separable, linear), simple second-order. <br> 8. **Stats inference**: Hypothesis testing, confidence intervals, p-values. <br> 9. **Python/NumPy for math**: Array ops, symbolic (SymPy), plotting (Matplotlib). | 📐 **Engineering modeler**: Solve and plot projectile motion ODEs; compute trajectories. <br> 🔍 **Data explorer**: Use NumPy to compute stats on a dataset; perform t-tests on A/B test results. <br> 🧩 **Project**: "Approximate π using Monte Carlo integration; compare to Taylor series." <br> 📊 **Assessment**: "Diagonalize a 3x3 matrix and explain its use in ML dimensionality reduction." |
